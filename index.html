A Points-Buy Advancement System for 2024 Dungeons & Dragons

DragonReach Forge

In PACT, everything your character is — levels, class features, spells, skills, proficiencies — is bought with a single currency: Advancement Points. There are no class tables and no automatic level-up packages. Your level is simply how many Hit Dice you have bought. Want to be a Wizard who took up the sword at forty? Buy the sword training. Want to stay a lightly-built scout who never gets tougher? Don't buy the Hit Dice.

What you're getting into

  • One currency. Advancement Points buy Hit Dice, features, spells, and proficiencies alike.
  • Level = Hit Dice. No class table decides what you get at 5th. You do.
  • Built on 2024 5e. Species, classes, and spells you already know, priced rather than granted.
  • Roughly 535 AP builds a full level-20 character on the standard track — 455 lean, 615 generous.
  • Still in playtest. The open questions are published, not hidden.
